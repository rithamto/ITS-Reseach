# Context-Aware Functional Security for V2X-Enabled Systems

**Published:** 2026
**Source:** IEEE Publications on Security and Safety

## Abstract
Security remains a top priority in the deployment of V2X communications, as malicious attacks can have life-threatening consequences. This paper introduces a "Context-Aware Functional Security" framework for V2X-enabled systems. The proposed architecture dynamically adjusts security protocols and authentication mechanisms based on the current context of the vehicle, such as its location, speed, traffic density, and the criticality of the communication. The study also explores the integration of blockchain and federated learning to secure vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) communications without compromising privacy.

## Key Findings
- A one-size-fits-all security approach is inefficient and potentially vulnerable in diverse V2X scenarios.
- Context-aware security dynamically balances the trade-off between security overhead (latency, computation) and the required level of protection.
- Blockchain technology, combined with federated learning, offers a decentralized and privacy-preserving method for sharing threat intelligence and authenticating messages among vehicles.
- The proposed framework significantly improves resilience against sophisticated cyber-attacks, such as spoofing and data injection, in connected vehicle networks.
