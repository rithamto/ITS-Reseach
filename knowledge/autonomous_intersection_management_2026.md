# Autonomous Intersection Management via Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

**Published:** 2026
**Source:** Markets and Markets / AI in Transportation Research

## Abstract
As the automotive industry shifts toward city-scale mobility intelligence, algorithmic coordination of traffic is becoming a reality. This research focuses on Autonomous Intersection Management (AIM) systems designed to replace traditional traffic signals. Leveraging V2X communication and multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL), the proposed system enables vehicles to negotiate right-of-way and navigate through intersections cooperatively. The study also incorporates cooperative perception, allowing vehicles to share sensor data (LiDAR, radar, camera) to detect vulnerable road users and occluded vehicles.

## Key Findings
- AIM systems using MARL can significantly reduce traffic congestion and vehicle wait times at intersections compared to traditional traffic lights.
- Cooperative perception is essential for the safety of AIM, providing vehicles with a comprehensive view of the intersection beyond their line-of-sight.
- The decentralized nature of MARL allows the system to scale effectively and handle varying traffic densities and complex intersection topologies.
- Real-time V2X communication with ultra-low latency is a prerequisite for the successful implementation of such coordinated maneuvers.
