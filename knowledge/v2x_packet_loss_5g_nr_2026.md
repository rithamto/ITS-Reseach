# Packet Loss Estimation in 5G NR V2X Networks

**Published:** 2026
**Source:** IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) / Academic Research

## Abstract
This paper explores the challenges and solutions related to packet loss estimation in 5G New Radio (NR) Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) networks. As connected and autonomous vehicles increasingly rely on ultra-reliable low-latency communication (URLLC), accurately estimating and mitigating packet loss becomes critical for safety and efficiency. The study presents novel algorithms for predicting packet loss in highly dynamic vehicular environments, leveraging machine learning techniques to adapt to rapidly changing channel conditions.

## Key Findings
- Traditional packet loss estimation methods fall short in the highly mobile and unpredictable environments of 5G NR V2X.
- Machine learning models, specifically deep reinforcement learning, can significantly improve the accuracy of packet loss predictions.
- The proposed algorithms enhance the overall reliability of V2V and V2I communications, paving the way for safer autonomous driving applications.
- The research highlights the need for continuous adaptation of network parameters based on real-time packet loss estimations.
