# 6G as the Next-Generation Foundation for Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS)

**Published:** 2026
**Source:** Semantic Scholar / Telecommunications Research

## Abstract
While 5G NR has brought significant advancements to V2X communications, the stringent requirements of fully autonomous driving and city-scale intelligent transportation systems demand even greater capabilities. This paper evaluates 6G as the foundation for the next generation of ITS, addressing the limitations of current LTE and 5G implementations. The study focuses on 6G's potential to deliver terabit-per-second data rates, sub-millisecond latency, and massive connectivity, which are essential for advanced use cases like holographic remote driving and cooperative perception.

## Key Findings
- 6G networks will integrate terrestrial and non-terrestrial (e.g., satellite, UAV) communication, providing seamless and ubiquitous coverage for vehicles.
- The integration of sensing, localization, and communication in 6G will enable centimeter-level positioning accuracy, crucial for autonomous navigation.
- Artificial intelligence will be natively embedded in the 6G network architecture, optimizing resource allocation and network management for vehicular applications.
- 6G is expected to overcome the capacity and latency bottlenecks of 5G, fully realizing the vision of connected and cooperative autonomous driving.
